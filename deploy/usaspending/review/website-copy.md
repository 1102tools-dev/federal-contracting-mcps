Include the tool name, approximate time, and error message. Do not send credentials or private acquisition information. Support is provided on a best-effort basis.

## Privacy
The service receives the tool name and parameters you provide and sends the corresponding data query to USAspending.gov. Do not include confidential information in queries. The integration does not require an account or API key and does not intentionally store query bodies, query results, or conversation history. Its application error logging is limited to generic availability events. Cloudflare processes connection metadata, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service and enforce request limits. USAspending and your AI client process data under their own policies. Operational pacing state contains request timing and provider cooldown information. Contact james@1102tools.com with privacy questions.

Before publication: verify Cloudflare account logging and retention settings and disclose any additional collection. This draft does not assert that infrastructure retains no metadata.

## Terms
The USAspending MCP is an independent tool provided by 1102tools for accessing publicly available spending information. It is not endorsed by USAspending.gov, the U.S. government, or OpenAI. Public data may be delayed, incomplete, corrected, or unavailable. Verify material findings against the source before relying on them. Access is subject to capacity limits and may be interrupted or suspended to protect service availability. The tool does not make procurement decisions or modify government records. Do not use the service to interfere with its operation or bypass access limits. The software is distributed under its repository's MIT license. Contact james@1102tools.com for support.
